# Popover

Use `Popover` for non-modal contextual content anchored to a trigger. The surrounding surface remains interactive and available to assistive technology; the popover has no dimmed scrim. Render it inside `BreezeProvider`.

Choose `Popover` rather than `Menu` when the content mixes information with controls, such as a list of notifications with their own actions beside an `AppearanceControl`. A `Menu` holds only a list of actions.

## Basic usage

`trigger` supplies the label for the button Breeze renders. `title` supplies the surface's visible heading and accessible name; `children` supplies its content. Breeze also renders a close button with a label from the provider's messages.

```tsx
<Popover title="Delivery details" trigger="View delivery details">
  <Typography>Your delivery details appear here.</Typography>
</Popover>
```

## Open state

By default, the component manages its own open state. Set `defaultOpen` to start open, and optionally use `onOpenChange` to observe changes.

To control the surface from application state, supply both `open` and `onOpenChange`. `open` and `defaultOpen` are mutually exclusive.

```tsx
<Popover
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  open={open}
  title="Delivery details"
  trigger="View delivery details"
>
  <Typography>Your delivery details appear here.</Typography>
</Popover>
```

## Dismissal

`dismissible` defaults to `true`, allowing Escape and interaction outside the surface to dismiss it. Set it to `false` when those interactions should leave the surface open. The close button remains available, so a `panel` cannot set it to `false`.

## Placement

`placement` accepts `top`, `bottom` (default), `start`, `end` or `bottom end`. `bottom end` opens below the trigger with their end edges aligned, for a trigger at the end of a bar. Start and end follow the provider's text direction. The surface can adjust its position to fit the available space.

## Panels

Set `variant` to `panel` for content that names its own purpose, such as an account panel of notifications and settings. A panel shows no title or close button; `title` still names it for assistive technology. Its content runs edge to edge with no padding, and it fits that content from a minimum of 336px, or spans the viewport less 12px on each side on narrow screens. A panel closes with Escape or a press outside it, so it cannot set `dismissible` to `false`.

Set `triggerInitials` to open the popover from a circular button showing a person's initials. `trigger` then supplies only the button's accessible name, so describe what it opens. Set `triggerIndicator` to add an attention dot, such as for unread notifications, and say so in `trigger`, for example "Account and notifications, 2 unread".

```tsx
<Popover
  placement="bottom end"
  title="Account and notifications"
  trigger="Account and notifications, 2 unread"
  triggerIndicator
  triggerInitials="MG"
  variant="panel"
>
  {/* Notifications, AppearanceControl and account links */}
</Popover>
```

## Accessibility and motion

Breeze manages focus on opening and restores it on closing without trapping focus inside the popover. The provider's portal host keeps the surface's locale, direction and scoped styles intact.

Inside a drawer, a popover appears above the sheet while its content remains interactive and accessible. The sheet keeps its scrim. Escape closes the popover first and restores focus to its trigger; a subsequent Escape closes the sheet. The `Foundations/Overlay stack` stories exercise this composition.

Enter and exit motion respects reduced-motion preferences. A closing surface immediately leaves the active stack while its exit animation completes.
