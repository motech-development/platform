# Button

Buttons let people perform an action, such as saving a record or submitting a form.

## Basic button

`Button` has four variants: `primary` (default), `secondary`, `quiet` and `danger`. `children` sets the visible label.

<Canvas of={Stories.Primary} />

## Secondary button

Secondary buttons are neutral alternatives to the primary action, such as cancelling.

<Canvas of={Stories.Secondary} />

## Quiet button

Quiet buttons are text-only actions with no border or fill, for actions that sit alongside content, such as **Mark all as read** in a panel header or **Download** on a row. They come in one size and do not accept `size`. Use a `Link` instead when the action navigates.

<Canvas of={Stories.RowAction} />

## Danger button

Danger buttons are destructive actions. Use a label that states what will be removed.

<Canvas of={Stories.Danger} />

## Sizes

`size` accepts `sm`, `md` (default) and `lg`, with minimum heights of 32, 36 and 52px. Small labels are 12px and the others 13px, all semibold. Sizes apply to the `primary`, `secondary` and `danger` variants.

## Icon

Set `icon` to show a Breeze icon before the label. It works with every variant and size, and is 15px at `size="sm"` and 16px at `md` and `lg`. While loading, the icon is hidden along with the label.

<Canvas of={Stories.WithIcon} />

## Loading

Set `loading` while the action is in progress. A placeholder replaces the label, and the button cannot be activated again until loading ends.

<Canvas of={Stories.Loading} />

## Disabled

`disabled` prevents activation and removes the button from the tab order.

<Canvas of={Stories.Disabled} />

## Handling actions

`onAction` is called with no arguments when the button is activated by pointer, touch or keyboard.

<Canvas of={Stories.Activation} />

## Form submission

Buttons default to `type="button"`. Set `type="submit"` to submit the surrounding form, and handle submission on the form. `form`, `name` and `value` are also supported.

## Accessibility

- The label is the button's accessible name, including while it is loading. Icons are decorative.
- On touch devices, buttons have a minimum 44px target. Quiet buttons have a minimum 44px height on touch devices and below 901px.
- `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`, `aria-describedby`, `aria-controls`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-haspopup` are supported.
