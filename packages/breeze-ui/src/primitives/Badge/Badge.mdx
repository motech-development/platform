# Badge

`Badge` shows a short, non-interactive status or count, such as "Paid" or "3".

## Variants

`variant` accepts `neutral`, `brand`, `positive`, `warning` and `danger`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Treatments} />

## Loading

Set `loading` to keep the badge's shape while its text is unavailable.

<Canvas of={Stories.Loading} />

## Accessibility

Make the text meaningful on its own, without relying on colour. Keep it short, and do not put controls inside a badge.
