# Menu

`Menu` presents a list of actions in a popover. Breeze owns the trigger,
positioning, focus management and overlay lifecycle.

<Canvas of={Stories.Default} />

## Descriptor content

Pass `items` and a `getItem` mapping function. The mapping returns the closed
`ItemDescriptor` contract: `id`, `label`, optional `description`, `icon`,
`badge` and `disabled`. Every `id` must be unique within the menu. The callback
receives the selected descriptor; item content cannot be supplied as JSX,
classes, styles or slot props.

## Visibility and actions

Use `defaultOpen` for an uncontrolled menu. For controlled visibility, provide
`open` and `onOpenChange` together. The action callback receives the descriptor
for the selected item and never a DOM event or the original application item.

<Canvas of={Stories.Controlled} />

## Keyboard and overlays

The trigger exposes menu semantics. Arrow keys open the menu and move through
enabled actions, type-to-search moves to matching labels, Enter or Space selects
an action, and Escape closes the menu and restores focus to its trigger. Inside
a Breeze drawer or dialog, the menu closes before the parent surface.

The open story is checked by Storybook's accessibility addon. The menu and its
trigger receive accessible names from their visible labels, and item
descriptions are associated with their action.
